How to Make Lumpia

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 cup finely chopped carrots
  • 1 cup chopped onions
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Lumpia wrappers
  • Cooking oil

Procedure

  1. In a bowl, combine ground pork, carrots, onions, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Mix all the ingredients thoroughly.
  3. Place a spoonful of mixture onto a lumpia wrapper.
  4. Roll and seal the wrapper tightly.
  5. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  6. Fry the lumpia until golden brown.
  7. Remove from oil and drain excess oil.
  8. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.
